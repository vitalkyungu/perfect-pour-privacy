Perfect Pour support

Hold to pour, release on the line.

How to play

Press and hold anywhere on the cup to pour. Let go on the dashed mint line. Dead on the line is a Perfect (+1 second); inside the green band is Great or Good. Overfill and it spills (−2 seconds). Every 3 hits in a row raise your multiplier, up to ×5. Everyone gets the same cups each day.

Leaderboard

The daily board needs an internet connection; the game itself works offline. To change your name, tap "Change name" on the start screen. To remove yourself, open "Privacy policy" in the app and tap "Remove me from the leaderboard".
