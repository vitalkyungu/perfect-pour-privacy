Perfect Pour privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

The short version

Perfect Pour has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it never sells your data. You never give us your name, email or phone number.

What the daily leaderboard stores

When you play online, the app creates a random anonymous player ID for this phone. No sign-up, email or password is involved.

With that ID we store the nickname you pick (or "Coffee fan"), your best score for the day, your best score ever, the date, and when it was last updated.

Your nickname and score are shown to every player on that day’s board. Please don’t use your real name if you’d rather stay anonymous.

Where it is kept

Leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which processes it only to run the leaderboard for us.

Your personal bests and nickname are also saved on this phone so the game works offline. They never leave the phone except as described above.

What we don’t collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising ID or device identifiers. No third-party SDKs for ads or analytics.

Your choices

In the app, open "Privacy policy" on the start screen and tap "Remove me from the leaderboard" to delete your scores and nickname from our server straight away. If you play again later, you’ll start fresh with a new anonymous ID. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Deleting the app removes everything stored on this phone.

Children

Perfect Pour is suitable for all ages and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from children. Nicknames are filtered for offensive words.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
